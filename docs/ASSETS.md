# Demo assets

- `games/demo`: generated checker texture and procedural/demo geometry.
  `environments/studio_small_09_1k.hdr`: Studio Small 09 by Sergej Majboroda,
  [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09), CC0.
  Powered by Poly Haven. Download receipt: 1,615,248 bytes,
  MD5 `d5d7eb9d26d341d6aa4d11c1a54fd97c`; fetch script checks this digest.
- `games/test_meadow`, `test_voxels`, `test_cloth`: procedural scenes, no downloaded packs.
- `games/showcase`: Kenney Retro Medieval Kit models (CC0), original procedural effects
  and synthesized WAV effects generated by `tools/make_showcase_audio.py`.
  Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/retro-medieval-kit . Attribution is appreciated.
- `games/village` and `games/test_fps` are advanced **local import fixtures**. Their
  source scenes/scripts are included; imported models/textures/music remain local and
  are excluded from Git. Regenerators in `crates/kvas_engine/examples` explain source paths.
  They are not complete portable demo downloads.
- `.test_assets` contains local downloaded packs and is never published by the repository.

Quaternius Medieval Village / Stylized Nature Standard license files supplied with
the local packs state CC0. Other packs have different conditions: for example the
PSX Kitchen source files prohibit redistribution. Do not assume one pack's license
applies to all import fixtures. SDK binaries and local recordings are also excluded.
