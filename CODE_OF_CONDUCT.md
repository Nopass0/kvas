# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Kvas community to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or communication style. We expect participants to act professionally and help keep collaboration constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Give actionable, technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them constructively.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and community boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct without consent, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Unwelcome conduct also includes repeated hostile behavior after a request to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing Kvas in public. Report conduct to the repository maintainers through GitHub's private contact/reporting facilities. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate action, including a warning, temporary restriction, or removal from project spaces. Maintainers must explain enforcement decisions when doing so does not compromise safety or privacy.
